# Listing Scoring Directive

## Philosophy

A short commute is worth real money. Do not rank only by rent.

15 minutes saved each way is about 30 minutes per workday, or about 10 hours per month. A more expensive room near Root D4 can be better than a cheaper room with a tiring commute.

## Commute Score

A+:
- under about 20 minutes door-to-door to PHENOGY Root D4
- either public transport or e-bike is strong
- apply immediately if other constraints pass

A:
- 20-30 minutes reliable commute
- apply same day

B:
- 30-45 minutes
- apply only if price, WG fit, or temporary value is strong

C:
- over 45 minutes
- skip unless emergency

## Price Score

Excellent:
- under CHF 800

Good:
- CHF 800-900

Acceptable:
- CHF 900-1000 with A/A+ commute or very strong WG fit

Weak:
- over CHF 1000, skip unless Simon explicitly approves

## WG Fit Signals

Positive:
- calm WG
- clean / tidy expectations
- young professionals
- mixed WG
- shared cooking or relaxed social vibe
- no party-WG wording
- furnished
- Anmeldung possible
- secure bike storage

Negative:
- party-heavy language
- unclear contract
- no viewing
- pressure tactics
- rent looks unrealistically low
- payment before viewing
- gender restriction excluding men

## Output

Each listing should have:
- URL
- source
- title
- rent
- city/area
- move-in date
- commute class
- gender eligibility
- scam risk
- recommended action
- generated message draft
